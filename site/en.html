Good to know before you go.

Book on Naver
Where do I book and pay?

Choose your date and time, book and pay through Naver Smart Place. Select the ‘Book on Naver’ button on this website. Opening schedules and availability follow the information on Naver.

Do adults who only take photos need to pay?

Yes. Every visitor, including accompanying adults, joins one paid experience. Children under 24 months can enter free with proof of age.

How many strawberries can I take home?

Each person receives one 500g picking pack. Please fill it so the lid closes completely. Extra picking is charged on site per 100g at that day's price.

Is the family photo session always available?

No. It is a complimentary surprise event, not a permanent service. Availability depends on farm operations and arriving at your booked time. Consent to the use of your likeness is required.

How do I receive an edited digital photo?

If your family has a photo session, you can receive an edited digital photo after posting a photo review on a designated channel: Naver Blog, a parenting community, Karrot Neighborhood Life or an Instagram feed post. Stories do not qualify. Writing a review is optional.

What safety information should I know?

Please do not disturb the pollinating bees. Contact us before booking if you have allergies to bees, strawberries or pollen. Tell a staff member immediately if you feel unwell. Please taste strawberries only in the designated area.

How do I change or cancel a booking?

Please check the change and cancellation policy on Naver Booking. Cancellations made by the farm are fully refunded. No-shows are not refundable.
